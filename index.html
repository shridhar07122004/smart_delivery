How the colony chooses

Each ant selects among unvisited checkpoints according to learned pheromone strength and distance-based visibility. Shorter tours deposit more pheromone, while evaporation gradually reduces older trail influence.

P(i → j) ∝ pheromone(i,j)α × visibility(i,j)β
visibility(i,j) = 1 / distance(i,j)

The optimizer reports the best route it discovers. It does not guarantee a globally optimal tour. Locations are synthetic points on an abstract 100 × 100 grid, so distances are not real-world kilometres.
